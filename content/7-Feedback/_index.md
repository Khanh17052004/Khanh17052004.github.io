### General Evaluation

**1. Working Environment** The working environment is highly friendly and open-minded. Every member at FCJ is always ready to support me whenever I face difficulties, even outside of official working hours. The workspace is neat and comfortable, which greatly helps me focus. However, I think adding a few more social gatherings or team-bonding activities would help everyone understand each other even better.

**2. Mentor & Team Admin Support** My mentor provided highly detailed guidance, explained things clearly when I didn't understand, and constantly encouraged me to ask questions. The team admin smoothly supported me with all procedures and documentation, creating the best conditions for my work. I especially appreciate that my mentor allowed me to try, experiment, and solve problems on my own instead of just handing out the answers.

**3. Alignment Between Work and Academic Major** The tasks assigned to me perfectly aligned with the knowledge I learned at university, while simultaneously expanding into new domains that I hadn't encountered before. Thanks to this, I was able to both consolidate my foundational knowledge and acquire practical, real-world skills.

**4. Learning Opportunities & Skill Development** During my internship, I picked up many new skills, such as utilizing project management tools, teamwork, and professional communication within a corporate environment. My mentor also shared a wealth of practical experience that has given me much better direction for my future career path.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit** The company culture is extremely positive: everyone respects one another, working seriously yet maintaining a cheerful atmosphere. When facing tight project deadlines, everyone pulls together and supports each other regardless of their position. This truly made me feel like an integral part of the collective team, even as an intern.

**6. Benefits & Policies for Interns** The company provides an internship allowance and offers flexible scheduling options whenever needed. Additionally, being able to participate in internal training sessions is a major plus point.

---

### Other Q&A

- **What are you most satisfied with during your internship?** My mentor's detailed guidance, clear explanations, and encouragement to ask questions. Along with that, the admin team's proactive support with paperwork and onboarding materials made my workflow seamless. I highly value the autonomy my mentor gave me to troubleshoot and resolve issues independently rather than just giving me the solutions directly.

- **What do you think the company could improve for future interns?** As shared in the environment section, the company could organize a few short social interactions, small knowledge-sharing sessions, or quick team-bonding activities between official members and interns. This would help future batches feel less overwhelmed during their first few days and integrate into the team much faster.

- **If recommending to friends, would you advise them to intern here? Why?** Absolutely, yes. First Cloud Journey is truly an excellent launching pad for students. Here, you don't just reinforce your specialized academic knowledge; you get hands-on experience in a real production environment, guided by dedicated mentors, and you are respected as a full team member.

---

### Proposals & Aspirations

- **Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?** I propose building a structured roadmap or a checklist of core skills and tools that interns should look into right from the first week (if available). This would allow interns to proactively prepare mentally and review foundational knowledge, making the transition into actual projects much smoother.

- **Would you like to continue with this program in the future?** Yes, I would highly look forward to it. If a suitable opportunity arises, I am more than willing to continue accompanying the team to dive deeper into specialized knowledge and contribute more to FCJ.

- **Other feedback / Free sharing:** Thank you to all the mentors, the admin team, and every member of FCJ for creating a truly meaningful and unforgettable "First Cloud Journey" for me. Wishing the program continuous growth and looking forward to welcoming even more high-quality generations of interns in the future!
